# Study Assistant (working name)

Upload a PDF and study it one short chunk at a time: a quick summary, flashcards, then a quiz before moving on.

## The Unique Kernel
Your document becomes a series of small study rounds instead of one long wall of text. Every chunk is **summary → flashcards → quiz**, and then you move to the next chunk. Most tools summarize the whole document at once. This one is built to keep you from losing focus.

## Who It's For
First, the learner. Later, anyone studying from long books or PDFs.
Today: they read the PDF straight through. "It's too long and boring so I lose focus," they get tired after a few pages, and they drop it.

## The Core Loop
1. Upload a PDF.
2. Read a short summary of the first chunk.
3. Go through flashcards for that chunk.
4. Take a quick quiz and see the score.
5. Hit **next**, and the next chunk starts.

The loop is the point: small wins keep you moving forward instead of quitting a few pages in.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
"When trying to study by reading a book or PDF, I get tired of it after a few pages so I end up dropping it. I want to use the study assistant to fix that and even more."

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a one-minute video: upload a PDF → see a short summary → flip through flashcards → take the quiz and see a score → press **next** and the second chunk appears with its own summary, flashcards and quiz.
The "oh, that's cool" moment: a long, boring PDF turns into bite-sized rounds you actually want to finish.

## The POC Boundary
- PDF upload
- Splitting the document into chunks
- For each chunk: short summary, flashcards, quiz
- A score for each quiz, plus a running score for the current study session
- Moving to the next chunk

## Later
- Tracking scores over time (needs saving, see below)
- User accounts and saving progress
- Opening it up for other people to use
- Other file types (Word docs, web articles)

## Explicitly Cut
- **Content idea generator.** It was the other candidate. We chose the study assistant because the learner lives that problem personally and can judge whether the fix works.
- **Accounts and saving for this version.** The learner chose to leave these out. They aren't needed to prove the chunk-by-chunk loop, and they would take up the 2–4 hour budget.
