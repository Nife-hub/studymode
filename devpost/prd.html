StudyMode

Turns a long PDF or pasted notes into short study rounds, one chunk at a time: summary, then flashcards, then a quiz.

From scope to product

Scope said “summary → flashcards → quiz, one chunk at a time.” Now we know exactly what that means: an opening page, a home page with steps and two ways in, a loading state, tap-to-flip cards, 5 multiple-choice questions per chunk with instant correct/wrong, flashcards you choose the number of (max 10) plus your own cards, a running score, and a finish line with your total.

Boundaries

What we're building
  • Opening Page → Home Page (hero, steps, PDF upload, paste text)
  • Loading icon while content is prepared
  • Per chunk: summary (no timer, create your own card) → your chosen number of flashcards, max 10, tap to flip → 5-question quiz with correct/wrong and a chunk score
  • Running session score, then a Finished Screen with the total and “upload another PDF”
  • Friendly error for unusable files
Deferred, and later ideas
  • Score history over time (needs saving)
  • Accounts and saved progress
  • Other file types (Word, web articles)
  • Opening it up publicly; resuming where you left off
Non-goals
  • Not a whole-document summarizer. It always works one chunk at a time.
  • Not the content idea generator (cut in scope).
  • No reading text out of scanned images. It shows an error and suggests pasting text.
Open questions (assumptions)
  • Chunk size: “short,” with no reading timer. The exact amount of text is decided in 4-spec.
  • Own-card template: assumed front (question or keyword) and back (your note).
