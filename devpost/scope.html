Scope · approved

Study Assistant (working name)

Upload a PDF and study it one short chunk at a time: a quick summary, flashcards, then a quiz before moving on.

The unique kernel

Your document becomes a series of small study rounds instead of one long wall of text. Most tools summarize everything at once. This one is built to keep you from losing focus.

The core loop

Who it's for

First, you. Later, anyone studying from long books or PDFs. Today you read the PDF straight through, and then:

“It's too long and boring so I lose focus… I get tired of it after a few pages so I end up dropping it.”

What “working” looks like · the one-minute demo

Upload a PDFShort summaryFlashcardsQuiz + scoreNext → chunk 2 appears

The “oh, that's cool” moment: a long, boring PDF turns into bite-sized rounds you actually want to finish.

What's in, what waits, what's out

Now: the proof of concept
  • PDF upload
  • Splitting the document into chunks
  • For each chunk: short summary, flashcards, quiz
  • A score for each quiz, plus a running score for this study session
  • Moving to the next chunk
Later: worth doing, not yet
  • Tracking scores over time (needs saving)
  • User accounts and saving progress
  • Opening it up for other people to use
  • Other file types (Word docs, web articles)
Cut, and why
  • Content idea generator. The other candidate idea. We chose the study assistant because you live this problem and can judge whether the fix works.
  • Accounts and saving for this version. Not needed to prove the chunk-by-chunk loop, and they'd eat into the 2–4 hour budget.
